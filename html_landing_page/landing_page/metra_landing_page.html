Why inspections vary so much

The Rules don't change. Applying them consistently is the hard part.

1

Inspection quality depends on the officer

Every officer knows the Legal Metrology Act. Almost no one has every clause of it fresh in mind while standing in a warehouse. A missed sub-rule or a forgotten exemption can mean a violation goes unflagged, or a compliant label gets questioned. METRA checks the same Rules, the same way, on every scan.

2

A clean shop is fast. A violation isn't.

Checking a shop with no issues takes about fifteen minutes. The moment a violation turns up, the visit can stretch into a full day - documenting it, filing the panchnama, checking the company's past record. METRA gives the officer accurate findings and the company's history immediately, and pre-fills the paperwork, so a violation slows the visit down far less.

Built for practical inspections

Everything you need in one compliance workspace

From scanning and rule reference to history and documentation, METRA brings the inspection workflow together.

⌕

Scan & analyse packages

Photograph a label and METRA reads the nine declarations the Rules require - manufacturer, net quantity, MRP, date of packing, country of origin, consumer care and more - and flags anything missing, wrongly formatted, or in too small a font.

✓

Rule-guided, not memory-guided

Every check traces back to a specific rule and section of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 - the same reference, applied the same way, whether it's an officer's first inspection or their thousandth.

▤

Reports that fill themselves in

Complainant details, the company being reported, and the specifics of the violation carry straight from the scan into the report - officers check it and file it, instead of retyping what METRA already found.

◷

A record for every company

Every mismatch is logged against the company, not just the scan. A pattern of violations follows a manufacturer across inspections and across officers.

Pre-market self-check

Vendors can run the same check on their own packaging before it ever reaches a shelf, and catch a labelling issue while it's still cheap to fix.

Simple workflow

From package to inspection record

Inspections start the way they always have - a planned route, a licence renewal, a complaint through the National Consumer Helpline, a tip-off, a festive-season drive, or a random check. METRA doesn't change when an inspection happens, only what happens once the officer is there.

1

Scan or upload

Capture a clear image of the product package or label using a camera or upload.

2

Analyse compliance

METRA checks the visible label information and highlights declarations that need attention.

3

Review & document

Review the result, use company history and prepare the relevant inspection documentation.

If a violation holds up, what follows - seizure or sealing, notice to the trader, prosecution, penalties - is the same process it's always been. METRA's part ends at giving the officer an accurate, well-documented starting point, and keeping the record for next time.

One platform, four dashboards

Designed around the people in the compliance ecosystem

Each dashboard gives its user exactly the level of access their role needs - nothing more.

Inspectors

The core of METRA: scan, review mismatches, check a company's history, and file a report with the details already filled in. Signing in requires a verified name, photograph, designation, government ID and state/region - only serving officers get this dashboard.
